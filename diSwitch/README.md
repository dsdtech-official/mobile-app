# diSwitch

diSwitch, formerly **DSD TECH SWITCH**, is a Bluetooth relay control app by
DSD TECH for Android and iOS. Compatible relay hardware is required.

## Downloads

| Platform | App download |
|---|---|
| Android | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.reb.switchbt) |
| iOS (iPhone and iPad) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1483733224) |

Install the store app for your platform. The sections below distinguish
Android and iOS features.

## Android features

- Scan for nearby devices and add compatible relays to **My devices**.
- View device details, connection status and available signal readings.
- Set device aliases and manage saved devices.
- Control relay channels and change the hardware device password on supported
  devices.
- Mark unrelated devices to exclude them from nearby scans, and restore them
  in **Settings**.

## iOS features

- Find and save nearby relay modules, inspect their connection status and
  switch relay channels from an iPhone or iPad.
- Manage connection credentials and hardware device passwords.
- Use English or Simplified Chinese, or follow the iOS language setting.

The iOS app requires compatible DSD TECH Bluetooth relay modules. Its device
records and settings are kept locally, and an app account is not required.

## Android compatibility and permissions

Compatible BLE relay hardware using the DSD profile is required. Available
controls depend on the connected device; this app does not control every
Bluetooth relay or Bluetooth device.

Bluetooth and location permissions may be required for scanning and connection,
depending on your Android version.

## Android saved devices and passwords

Saved device details and hardware passwords are stored locally. Android system
backup or device transfer may copy this data according to your system settings.

Removing a device from **My devices** deletes its saved local record; it does
not reset the physical relay. Marking a device as unrelated excludes it from
nearby scans and lets you restore it in **Settings**.
